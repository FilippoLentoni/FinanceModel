# Proposal

## Why
News sensitivity is not an out-of-sample performance experiment, and existing saved paper capital is not the requested separate $100 study. We need runnable matched forward research and explicit profile isolation.

## What Changes
- Add a runnable matched price-only versus news-enriched optimizer paper experiment with frozen prices, common initial holdings, constraints and transaction costs.
- Require vintage and publication/availability evidence before historical performance claims; return explicit coverage blockers when absent.
- Add separate caller-bound $100 VOO/GOOGL paper profile initialization through existing lifecycle APIs, preserving existing portfolios.
- Keep PPO/LLM policy eligibility honest and use existing news feature adapters.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `matched-news-paper-experiments`: Frozen matched research runs and isolated synthetic capital profiles.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
FinanceModel local experiment runner and paper lifecycle client; existing contract 1.8 APIs remain unchanged. Linked FinanceAgent change complete-etf-news-and-paper-beta owns hosted deployment and paid collection. No cloud training, live orders or real-money transfers.

## Selected daily universe (October 17, 2026)
The user selected GOOGL, AMZN, NVDA, MSFT, AAPL, META, TSLA and the S&P 500.
Represent the index using VOO, not an orderable index ticker. Collect eight
separate symbol-scoped news searches per trading day and persist raw/curated
records centrally. Preserve the $10 cumulative data limit and current authorized
expiry. Observed expected cost is $0.088/day, $0.88 for ten sessions; quotes
remain authoritative. Keep old receipts and their original symbol scope.

assets in two four-symbol batches. This does not constitute an eight-asset PPO
training integration. Aligned approved price ingestion for all eight assets,
explicit daily collection/no-news/failure status, compatible multi-batch PPO
state integration and deployed recommendation evidence remain required. Do not
claim an old two-asset profile or policy covers the new investment universe.
