## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Original current-book binding and approval
The trusted beta producer SHALL verify genuine operator identity, original captured input/shared source, model revision/native first-role messages and current isolated portfolio revision. Public submission SHALL NOT enable forward mode through an untrusted objective. A submitted request SHALL await independent GPU approval under existing budgets and expire after one hour.

#### Scenario: Book or source changes
- **WHEN** the original holdings, source checksum, model identity or availability cutoff no longer matches
- **THEN** the proposal or result reuse is rejected before creating compute or changing a ledger

### Requirement: Exact offline role evidence
The approved worker SHALL branch before historical simulation and invoke one native current-book swarm. Before each role it SHALL fsync the exact actual messages and permanent claim; before dependent roles it SHALL fsync the actual public response. Model/image/source/offline code checksums and independent approval SHALL remain bound to the evidence. No role output or hidden reasoning SHALL be invented.

#### Scenario: A backend fails after capture
- **WHEN** a role backend raises even a JSON-like ValueError after its input is claimed
- **THEN** the run is failed or indeterminate, the actual partial inputs/public responses are retained, and no successful hold or automatic reinvocation is claimed

#### Scenario: Container output is lost
- **WHEN** a started offline job has no verified complete exported result
- **THEN** it remains indeterminate/unusable, missing evidence is visible, and its permanent context prevents another automatic GPU job or regenerated messages

### Requirement: Approved native result reuse and honest costs
Hourly reuse SHALL require a completed independently approved result for the exact source/book/native context. Every dependent role message SHALL match original response-driven native construction. Original unavailable hourly inputs SHALL remain immutable. Costs SHALL be persisted once per managed job, with GPU observed billing unavailable unless supplied and external vendor cost zero for the network-isolated local model.

#### Scenario: Reuse within native cadence
- **WHEN** a verified original output is at most one day old and the source/book/context is unchanged
- **THEN** the adapter captures the original actual role contents and policy as an explicit reused signal without another model invocation or ledger write
