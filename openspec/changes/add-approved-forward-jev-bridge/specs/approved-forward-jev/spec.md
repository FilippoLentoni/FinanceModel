## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Authoritative current-book preparation
The trusted beta producer SHALL verify the genuine AWS caller and original immutable captured input, shared source, experiment, approach and isolated synthetic portfolio revision before proposing a single managed Jev forward decision. Public job submissions SHALL NOT activate this internal mode by supplying an objective alone.

#### Scenario: Forged or stale preparation
- **WHEN** a proposal supplies different original IDs, source checksum, book ownership, revision or a future-available source
- **THEN** the producer rejects it before creating a managed job or vendor invocation

### Requirement: Independent approval and permanent invocation recovery
Every managed forward decision SHALL preserve existing budget and independent human approval controls, immutable worker image identity, a permanent deterministic job identity and a conditional paid-invocation claim. An unknown paid outcome SHALL NOT be retried automatically. A captured response SHALL be recovered with the original invocation input without another provider call.

#### Scenario: Crash after a public response is captured
- **WHEN** the worker restarts after sealing its response but before completing its policy artifact
- **THEN** it validates and reuses the original response and input receipts without invoking the vendor again

#### Scenario: Fresh request after definite non-invocation
- **WHEN** a prior exact-context request expired without starting compute or has a verified not-invoked receipt
- **THEN** a fresh separately approved binding can conditionally advance the authoritative context head while original evidence remains permanent

### Requirement: Honest signal reuse and cost evidence
Hourly reuse SHALL require an approved completed matching native source/request/book/model context, disclose original worker invocation/source/model identity and leave original unavailable decisions immutable. Managed costs SHALL remain independently queryable even if no result is reused, with observed billing separated from estimates and no duplicated charge across reused rows.

#### Scenario: An expired or failed result is never used
- **WHEN** a managed attempt terminates without a usable hourly signal
- **THEN** its immutable cost event is transactionally persisted, imported once to ancillary reporting evidence and queued for the actual reporting-day revision without changing portfolio ledger records
