# Current-day premarket research state

The released `recursive_ppo_gdelt_premarket` profile uses actual GDELT GKG2.1 numeric organization metadata in four archives timestamped 07:45, 08:00, 08:15, and 08:30 America/New_York. The state is formed at 09:00, using the latest completed price history and only metadata actually eligible by that cutoff. A noon request returns that day's morning formation; its serving time is separately recorded. Before 09:00 it is not due. Weekend formations disclose the last completed market session and do not submit trades.

This is a separate source version (`gdelt-gkg-v2-premarket-organization-tone-v1`) and state group (`gdelt_intraday_organization_tone_v1`). A daily V1 actor cannot consume this source. Training and serving share the extractor and source window. Five inputs per asset are whole-document tone/100, log-scaled mention and cluster counts, window age/90 minutes, and effective source coverage (captured archive fraction multiplied by accepted physical-row fraction). Counts are bounded with log1p normalization by log1p(10000). Healthy zero mentions have coverage one; absent archives have unknown raw counts and tone, imputed zeros only alongside coverage zero. Partial counts are lower bounds. Four captured archives with rejected physical rows remain partial, with retained raw checksums and quarantine row evidence. Their accepted tone mean can be biased and counts are lower bounds. Live serving fails closed if every archive is absent or no row is usable or central capture occurred after the frozen cutoff. Historical source availability is an explicitly retrospective archive proxy; actual retrieval and central timestamps are preserved, never backdated.

Chronological periods are frozen before training: 2024-01-01 through 2025-05-31 training, 2025-07-02 through 2025-12-31 validation, and 2026 development evaluation. The June2025 interval flagged by the daily V1 archive audit is conservatively excluded between training and validation, without dropping market rows or inventing transitions. V2 source quality is audited independently. Corpus readiness requires actual XNYS session dates in the frozen splits from the checksum-verified snapshot; unused historical weekends and the excluded interval are not missing training states. Live collection still runs every calendar day for informational closed-market previews. Isolated incomplete windows retain their coverage mask and real HTTP attempt evidence. Previously inspected 2026 paths remain development data even under a new snapshot checksum. All five seeds, 30,000 maximum steps each, validation early stopping, trading costs, delayed open execution, portfolio weights, cash, drawdown, and the common evaluator remain explicit. No production promotion follows development evidence.

Classical daily eight-asset optimizers use the same eligible current-day numeric source for a disclosed bounded sensitivity calculation. Its fixed tone and mention coefficients are not estimated forecasts or evidence of predictive improvement. The model-selection historical classical baselines remain price-only controls; this input difference must be shown in comparisons. PPO consumes the source only after an actual matching corpus fit and actor export succeeds. All source documents, checksums, attempts, rights and availability provenance are retained for replay and recurring training. Automatic daily promotion is disabled.

GDELT whole-document tone is not calibrated stock sentiment; archive timestamps describe metadata processing rather than proof of article publication time. Exact organization aliases reduce ambiguity but do not establish relevance. VOO uses Vanguard mentions, not constituent-weighted ETF news. The implementation does not claim to reproduce state-of-the-art predictive performance.

Issued-decision comparisons distinguish formation time from completed price date and serving time. Two actual eligible news formations may share Friday's prices. Alignment records the actual previous/current formation, source protocol, document checksum, source window, holdings and model changes. Reversed news formations are rejected; same-cutoff cross-method comparisons remain valid. Identical-state replay can return zero differences for reproducibility and is explicitly labelled, never selected as newly observed temporal news history. Changing the news protocol is a model-input change and cannot be called a controlled temporal news comparison.


The separately released `recursive_ppo_gdelt_premarket_price_control` is an exact clone of the news study with only its40 news inputs removed. The58-dimensional price-only actor shares dates, five seeds, network, validation selection, rewards, costs, constraints and morning formation. It runs under a separate bounded producer-preflighted job after the news fit/export; it cannot acquire production eligibility from reused2026 data. Old2013–2024 price-study results are not an incremental-news ablation. An observed news effect can only be assessed after both actual matched studies complete, and never establishes causal market impact or guaranteed future returns. The news candidate remains explicitly exportable regardless of which hypothesis scores higher.

Full historical source capture runs in bounded CPU setup before any learner starts. The API preflight validates the registered configuration and budget and explicitly returns `source_freeze_pending_cpu_setup`; it does not claim that a corpus has been captured. Acceptance binds the approved price snapshot and manifest checksum, registered splits, instruments, source version and server cutoff. Idempotent replay returns that same accepted identity. CPU setup derives the actual required session dates from verified prices, reads at most two source requests concurrently, rejects central capture or retrieval after the accepted inventory cutoff, and persists the verified corpus plus a realized protocol manifest. This setup counts toward the existing 3,000-second manual job limit. A source or deadline failure produces no deployable policy. Export verifies the immutable accepted, realized and evaluated identities, retains the corpus checksum and source-manifest references, and never rewrites the accepted run specification.
