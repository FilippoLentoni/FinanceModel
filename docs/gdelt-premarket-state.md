# Current-day premarket research state

The released `recursive_ppo_gdelt_premarket` profile uses actual GDELT GKG2.1 numeric organization metadata in four archives timestamped 07:45, 08:00, 08:15, and 08:30 America/New_York. The state is formed at 09:00, using the latest completed price history and only metadata actually eligible by that cutoff. A noon request returns that day's morning formation; its serving time is separately recorded. Before 09:00 it is not due. Weekend formations disclose the last completed market session and do not submit trades.

This is a separate source version (`gdelt-gkg-v2-premarket-organization-tone-v1`) and state group (`gdelt_intraday_organization_tone_v1`). A daily V1 actor cannot consume this source. Training and serving share the extractor and source window. Five inputs per asset are whole-document tone/100, log-scaled mention and cluster counts, window age/90 minutes, and observed archive coverage fraction. Counts are bounded with log1p normalization by log1p(10000). Healthy zero mentions have coverage one; absent archives have unknown raw counts and tone, imputed zeros only alongside coverage zero. Partial counts are lower bounds. Live serving fails closed if every archive is absent or central capture occurred after the frozen cutoff. Historical source availability is an explicitly retrospective archive proxy; actual retrieval and central timestamps are preserved, never backdated.

Chronological periods are frozen before training: 2024-01-01 through 2025-05-31 training, 2025-07-02 through 2025-12-31 validation, and 2026 development evaluation. The major June2025 source outage is excluded between training and validation, without dropping market rows or inventing transitions. Isolated incomplete windows retain their coverage mask and real HTTP attempt evidence. Previously inspected 2026 paths remain development data even under a new snapshot checksum. All five seeds, 30,000 maximum steps each, validation early stopping, trading costs, delayed open execution, portfolio weights, cash, drawdown, and the common evaluator remain explicit. No production promotion follows development evidence.

Classical daily eight-asset optimizers use the same eligible current-day numeric source for a disclosed bounded sensitivity calculation. Its fixed tone and mention coefficients are not estimated forecasts or evidence of predictive improvement. The model-selection historical classical baselines remain price-only controls; this input difference must be shown in comparisons. PPO consumes the source only after an actual matching corpus fit and actor export succeeds. All source documents, checksums, attempts, rights and availability provenance are retained for replay and recurring training. Automatic daily promotion is disabled.

GDELT whole-document tone is not calibrated stock sentiment; archive timestamps describe metadata processing rather than proof of article publication time. Exact organization aliases reduce ambiguity but do not establish relevance. VOO uses Vanguard mentions, not constituent-weighted ETF news. The implementation does not claim to reproduce state-of-the-art predictive performance.
