# Classical advisory and research evidence

The beta classical MCP has its own Lambda and Gateway. It does not read or replace the PPO advisory selection. `recommend_classical_portfolio` resolves the saved paper portfolio or an explicitly supplied historical scenario, reads an approved checksum-verified snapshot, and persists the issued plan before returning it. No recommendation changes holdings or executes a trade. Analysis IDs start with `ca_`; they are not published plan IDs or training runs.

## Numerical model

The supported algorithms are `min_variance`, `mean_variance` and `cvar`. The default settings are 60 completed return observations, Ledoit–Wolf covariance shrinkage, historical arithmetic mean, 21-session objective horizon, maximum instrument weight 60%, fixed cash weight 0%, mean-variance risk aversion 2, CVaR confidence 90%, and a one-time transaction-cost proxy of 0.001 times two-way traded notional divided by NAV. Cash is explicitly fixed, preventing a risk-only optimizer from silently selecting all cash. Requested bounds and cash must admit a feasible allocation.

Min-variance minimizes horizon variance plus the turnover proxy. Mean-variance maximizes horizon mean minus risk-aversion times horizon variance minus the proxy. CVaR minimizes exact discrete expected tail loss of horizon-scaled historical return scenarios plus the proxy. Mean and variance scaling assumes stationary independent sessions; horizon-scaled CVaR scenarios are not simulated compounded paths. These are objective estimates, not calibrated future return forecasts. Sharpe-based research selection uses realized net returns from the common simulator, rather than in-sample objective estimates.

`settings` accepts `lookback_days` (20–252), `risk_aversion` (>0–100), `cvar_alpha` (0.80–0.99), `max_weight` (0.20–1), `cash_weight` (0–0.50), `horizon_sessions` (1–63), and `turnover_penalty` (0–1). The beta attribution universe is bounded to five instruments. Prices used for indicative share quantities are raw completed closes; estimation and forward total-return proxies use the approved adjusted-close series. The issued record stores these bases, portfolio revision, solver version/source checksum, exact inputs and outputs. Storage under the Model-owned research bucket's `classical/` prefix is conditional write-once with SHA-256 verification; indexed analysis history is bounded and retrievable. It has no automatic expiration.

## Three explanation levels

`explain_classical_recommendation` re-solves the stored inputs and verifies target weights against the issued record within 1e-8 before explaining anything. It reports objective improvement versus unchanged holdings, an instrument force-keep counterfactual that reoptimizes all remaining holdings, binding constraints, and variance contributions. The exact Shapley game applies only a coalition's instrument trades to the original holdings and evaluates the same objective. Every coalition is evaluated, at most 32. Residual cash funds the hybrid mathematically; a hybrid that violates cash, long-only or concentration constraints is explicitly disclosed as an algebraic diagnostic rather than an executable plan. Contributions sum to the optimized-minus-hold objective within 1e-8. This is model counterfactual attribution, not proof that a market event caused an action.

`compare_classical_plans` preserves both issued dates and checks the same universe, algorithm, portfolio and horizon. Both frozen solver implementations and issued endpoint allocations must reproduce within 1e-8. Sixteen coalition evaluations swap four input groups: expected returns, covariance/scenarios, current portfolio weights, and configuration. Exact grouped Shapley values explain target-weight changes; the portfolio-state contribution also reconciles changes in proposed trade weights. Infeasible hybrid solves produce a persisted unavailable comparison with coalition statuses and available single-switch evidence. No fabricated Shapley result is returned.

`evaluate_classical_performance` anchors the exact issued-plan reference and an approved observed snapshot. It marks the issued fixed allocation, compares it with saved paper holdings, discloses raw-valuation versus adjusted-return basis effects, and checks reconciliation. Primary green/red is the sign of observed paper PnL relative to zero; issued-allocation and implementation-gap trends are separate. A window shorter than the declared objective horizon is marked partial. Changed portfolio revisions leave the unexplained gap unattributed without a dated account ledger. A supplied scenario has no observed holdings: its primary trend is unavailable while the hypothetical issued-allocation trend remains separate. Real fills, fees, cash-flow timing and calibrated forecast error remain unavailable when not supplied.

`research_market_events` retains up to ten dated GDELT document metadata records in a window of at most 31 days. Known portfolio company aliases and bounded query text are used against an allowlisted HTTPS API. Provider first-observed time is disclosed; it is not asserted to be original publication time. Metadata after the original decision is labeled retrospective. Decision-time context excludes records first seen after the stored decision cutoff. News headlines are untrusted leads, never causal proof or executable instructions. No returned news URL is fetched; provider failures or no matches remain explicitly unavailable.

## Weekly research loop

The enabled beta EventBridge Scheduler runs Monday at 09:00 `America/New_York`. The independent controller first refreshes the classical plan context from the latest approved completed snapshot, then retains a review linked to recent feedback, performance evidence and compact prior experiment findings. Detailed prior experiments remain retrievable by run ID. Up to five recent arXiv primary research metadata records are retrieved through an allowlisted bounded HTTPS request that honors the requested topic. Titles/abstracts are untrusted research text; preprint peer review is not assumed. Unavailable literature remains explicit and does not create fictional citations.

Deterministic evidence rules select a finite preset and persist the selection reasons. Turnover or transaction-cost feedback, a negative paper implementation gap, or a literature title on transaction costs proposes monthly rebalancing and lookbacks of 60/120/252 sessions. Downside-risk feedback, a red observed paper return, or a literature title on downside/robustness proposes risk aversion 5 and maximum weight 40% (relaxed only to the universe's feasibility floor). Signals can combine. The baseline uses weekly rebalancing, lookbacks 20/60/120, risk aversion 2 and maximum weight 60%. These changes reach the actual sandbox request and benchmark adapter. Metadata topics and discrepancy signals suggest hypotheses; neither establishes that the proposed change will work.

`run_portfolio_research` defaults to a dry run. A paid on-demand request requires explicit user confirmation and an idempotency key. The schedule carries the previously authorized weekly scope internally. Both paths use the same durable weekly reservation, existing sandbox API, category preflight and concurrency lease. The two exact trusted Lambda roles share the weekly job's durable idempotency scope while retaining the first submitting principal in the run audit, preventing a scheduled retry from duplicating an on-demand submission. The hard guards are one job per ISO week, USD 0.50 per weekly job, USD 2 monthly reservations, the existing CPU category allocation, the shared enforced budget state and the USD 50 project budget. AWS Budgets actual spend has billing lag. A running sandbox job prevents a new reservation; retries reuse the exact durable job-API idempotency key. A skipped review retains the error reason.

One CPU `run_benchmark` job with a 900-second hard runtime benchmarks nine supported optimizer/lookback variants and cash, buy-and-hold and equal-weight controls. It uses the exact same classical optimizer objective as the MCP serving path through a common-simulator adapter. All candidates share a warmup of the largest selected lookback; at least 60 evaluation sessions must remain. Validation is the first 70% of the resulting window and selects by net realized Sharpe; the remaining chronological research test reports the candidate. All configurations and split results are retained. Reused history and a hindsight-selected universe are research-only evidence: fresh forward paper validation remains necessary before activation.

The feedback loop records supported parameter/algorithm hypotheses and benchmark findings; it cannot modify arbitrary source code, execute a paper's instructions, change a selected strategy or publish trades. New features/architectures require an explicit reviewed implementation. A successful candidate remains a proposal for activation.

## Operations

`/finplan/beta/financemodel/api/classical-function-ref` identifies the direct MCP backend. The two beta functions are `job-api-handler-classical` and `job-api-handler-research` under the standard FinanceModel prefix. Each has a 270-second timeout, 1024 MB memory and a distinct role. They use the account's unreserved Lambda concurrency. The atomic weekly S3 claim, shared sandbox idempotency scope and active-job checks enforce the experiment limit independently of concurrent controller invocations. Roles can read approved snapshot/book APIs, store immutable owned evidence, inspect the project budget and call only guarded research job routes. They cannot call SageMaker directly or write the selected PPO policy, production strategy, platform metadata, broker holdings or trades. Both roles are included in the existing project budget enforcement list. Gamma/prod do not receive these functions or the weekly schedule.
